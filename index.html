About me

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence at Sun Yat-sen University's School of Electronics and Communication Engineering through a joint doctoral training program with Shenzhen Loop Area Institute (SLAI). I am advised by Prof. Yulan Guo, who holds appointments at both institutions.

My research is primarily based in the Center for Embodied Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision (EACV) at SLAI. I am also a member of the Spatial Artificial Intelligence Lab (SAIL) at Sun Yat-sen University.

I study how robots can learn from experience, reason with world models, and adapt to a changing world. Previously, I worked on robotics algorithms at RoboSense and received my master's degree from Tsinghua University. I enjoy bringing research ideas through method design, code, and experiments to working robotic systems.

I welcome conversations about robot learning, research collaborations, and research internships. If our interests overlap, I would be happy to connect. Get in touch
