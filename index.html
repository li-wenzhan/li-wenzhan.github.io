A little background

From understanding motion
to learning intelligent action.

I am a Ph.D. student at Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Yulan Guo, with joint doctoral training at Shenzhen Loop Area Institute (SLAI). My research lies at the intersection of embodied AI, world-action models, and robot learning.

Previously, I worked on robotics algorithms at RoboSense and received my master's degree from Tsinghua University. I enjoy bringing research ideas through method design, code, and experiments to working robotic systems.
